Good to know

Frequently asked
questions.

Is ThumbCatch really free?+

Yes. It is completely free to use, with no accounts, usage limits, or hidden fees.

Which YouTube links are supported?+

Standard videos, youtu.be links, Shorts, Live, and embed URLs are all supported.

Why isn't the max-resolution image available?+

Some older or low-resolution videos do not have a max-resolution thumbnail. ThumbCatch automatically falls back to the best available image.

Is downloading thumbnails allowed?+

Thumbnails may be copyrighted. Use images you own or have permission to use, and follow YouTube's terms.
